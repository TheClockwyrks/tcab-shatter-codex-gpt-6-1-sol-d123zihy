# Shatter

A field of armored rock. One ship under pure momentum. A bright star at the
center of everything. Turn, burn, and carve your way through the debris in
Shatter, a space shooter where every shot has a curve to it.

Large rocks take punishment before breaking into two smaller threats. Break
those down again, finish the fragments, and earn a breath before the next wave
arrives faster and thicker. Damage glows through cracked armor, so you can pick
which target to finish first.

The gravity well pulls your rounds and the rocks, but never your ship. Let it
bend a shot around the star, or send a torpedo straight through the pull. Your
Warhead seeks the nearest target in its narrow forward cone and breaks any rock
outright. With ten seconds between charges, the moment you choose matters.

Keep moving when the saucer arrives. Its aimed shots join the churn, and a rock
swallowed by the star returns from the edge rather than disappearing. Survive,
make the well work for you, and see how far your three ships can take you.
